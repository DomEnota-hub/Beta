# Atlas Stage 4 — source-backed pair bindings

The [candidate binding inventory](../content-packs/locomotive-migration/atlas-pair-bindings.candidate.json) joins 72 preserved training sequences to their 72 display layouts and model equipment: 26 Ermak, 29 CHME3-family and 17 TEM2-family pairs. It records 299 positioned nodes and their exact equipment targets. The source objects themselves remain in their model-owned packs and are checked by their canonical JSON hashes. `qa/reconcile_atlas_pair_bindings.py --check` verifies identities, variant-qualified source-to-canonical mappings, source-scheme context, step anchors, edge endpoints, positive layout bounds and equipment target types. Unknown or cross-variant targets fail rather than being guessed.

The shared `AtlasCandidatePairLoader`, route and screen can present a stepwise functional preview with selectable display nodes and linked equipment cards. A missing actual variant disables the equipment transition. The candidate screen is **not registered in the Android navigation index** and the binding inventory says `runtimeImport=false`: this stage supplies a checked connection and reusable renderer, not publication approval. The existing VL80S screen and its separately checked 15 layout hotspots remain on their current path.

The displayed coordinates are for the training layout, not physical equipment locations or a wiring/piping diagram. For Ermak, a source scheme entry can be joined, but the underlying interactive diagram and exact 2ЭС5К/3ЭС5К execution applicability remain unresolved. For CHME3, the raw source-scheme references are preserved in documentary indexes but these pairs have no uniquely established canonical scheme target; their context target stays null. TEM2 source-scheme references join to variant-specific knowledge cards, not to an invented drawing. Historical claims of interactive view/hotspot/edge totals are not promoted to current runtime evidence.
