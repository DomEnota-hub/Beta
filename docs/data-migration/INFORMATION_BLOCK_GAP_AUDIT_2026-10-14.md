### Agreed boundary for later diagnostic work

The current migration phase completes source preservation and object accounting first. Diagnostic behavior and display are addressed after all informational blocks have been transferred. The extended corpus can intentionally contain archived instructions, driver experience and personal notes: **current normative confirmation is not a prerequisite for retaining or showing such material as clearly attributed information**. Its future visibility belongs behind explicit extended-mode enablement and a warning, with provenance and execution context visible. Historical or personal material must not silently become a current requirement or an executable action; action permission is a separate review axis. Thus `DEFERRED_SCOPE` here means these source objects are not wired into the present runtime, not that they are permanently disallowed from the future extended mode.
